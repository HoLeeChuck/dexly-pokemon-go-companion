# CatchGrid public-launch closure matrix

**Reviewed:** August 13, 2026

**Source audit:** [`docs/PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AUDIT.md`](PUBLIC_LAUNCH_AUDIT.md)
**Working catalog:** `2026-08-13.1`

This matrix accounts for every P0 and P1 finding in the source audit. Statuses describe the
reviewed source plus the linked staging and production evidence:

- `fixed` means the implementation and cited local evidence are present in the working tree.
- `open` means required implementation, automated evidence, or release verification is still
  missing.
- `externally blocked` means completion requires evidence, permission, or measurement that
  cannot be produced from the repository alone.
- `deliberately deferred` means the work must remain pending for a documented timing or migration
  reason.

The reviewed Phase B source is merged to protected public `main` and is live in isolated staging
and production with exact-SHA, migration, rollback-anchor, API/header/cache, browser, and
legacy-origin evidence recorded in
[`RELEASE_EVIDENCE_2026-08-13.md`](RELEASE_EVIDENCE_2026-08-13.md). The broad-launch
recommendation remains **no-go** until operational alerts and the external evidence below are
resolved; deployment alone does not close them.

## Remaining release gates

The matrix leaves one finding open, three externally blocked, and two deliberately deferred.
Before changing the recommendation to go, the release owner must at minimum:

1. configure and evidence Cloudflare alerts/dashboards, operational ownership, and an incident
   drill for Worker errors/latency, D1 errors/rows read, catalog cache behavior, and private-route
   rate limiting;
2. obtain an accepted asset-rights position or replacement assets;
3. record manual VoiceOver, keyboard, 200% zoom, contrast, reduced-motion, and all-theme review;
   and
4. collect trustworthy production field LCP, INP, and CLS evidence rather than inferring it from
   synthetic smoke or bundle size.
