## Product decisions that must be preserved

- CatchGrid is local-first. Public users store collection data in browser storage and do not need an account or shared cloud database.
- The private `/cody` owner mode may continue using D1, but it must remain unlisted, strongly authenticated, rate-limited, recoverable, and separate from the public local profile.
- Do not reintroduce the removed Trade page, Wanted tabs, For Trade tabs, or wanted-trade Search Lab mode.
- Do not add multi-user accounts, a trading board, Discord OAuth, a Discord bot, public profiles, or community listings. Those belong to a later community phase.
- Keep Home, Dex, Search Lab, and Profile as the public navigation.
- Pokémon detail collection states must remain whole-card buttons with clear collected/missing highlighting, accessible pressed semantics, and no small checkbox-style switches.
- Standard/default Pokémon continue to support Normal, Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, XXL, XXS, Shadow, and Purified when eligible.
- Alternate forms in the Phase B form gallery track only **Regular/Caught** and **Shiny**. Do not create Lucky, Hundo, size, Shadow, or Purified form checklists unless a later product decision explicitly asks for them.
- Form progress must not inflate National Dex species totals or regional species medals.
- Shadows cannot be traded. Mythicals must remain Lucky-ineligible and nontradeable except for verified current exceptions.
- Every Pokémon GO search generated by CatchGrid must begin with `!traded&` unless an authoritative syntax rule makes that inappropriate and the exception is documented and tested.
- The canonical public URL is `https://dex.cjdev.app`.
- Keep the current CatchGrid brand and theme system.

## Phase B definition

Phase B is the **form-aware complete collector toolkit**, adapted from the original Phase 2 roadmap and the decisions above.

It includes:

1. A versioned full National Dex species catalog that includes unreleased species as unavailable placeholders.
2. Released regional forms and meaningful alternate forms.
3. All 28 Unown forms.
4. Released Mega Evolutions and Primals.
5. Released Gigantamax forms and a model that can later represent Dynamax eligibility without duplicating species progress.
6. Released costumes, gender differences, Rotom-style forms, fused/alternate forms, and other collector-relevant Pokémon GO forms where reliable metadata and assets exist.
7. A Pokémon detail form gallery or expandable Forms tab. It must show each form's sprite, release state, Regular/Caught state, and Shiny state.
8. Form-aware CSV import/export and full portable backup/restore.
9. A flexible CSV column-mapping flow or a documented, test-covered adapter architecture if the complete wizard must be delivered in a later minor release.
10. Search Lab resources, a visual search builder, saved searches, and collection-aware recommendations.
11. Existing Discord-ready text sharing, plus safe Web Share support and optional QR/share-image output if it can be delivered accessibly without adding an account system.
12. Installable PWA/offline improvements, a controlled sprite fallback/cache strategy, and a reviewable catalog update center.

It does not include the removed trading UI or the Phase 3 shared-community features.
