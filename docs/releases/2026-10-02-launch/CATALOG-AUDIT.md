# Phase L1 catalog truth audit

Reviewed **2026-09-30**, on local branch **prism-forward**, starting at **d2aa25f**. Scope is Round 3 L1 only. This is an official-source review with conservative availability decisions, awaiting Claude. It does not authorize L2–L4 or a release.

## Result and evidence policy

Recomputed the 73 default species from the immutable generated catalog. Four have sourced availability: Toxel, Sinistea, Maschiff and Mabosstiff. Bramblin has an official future debut. The other 68 lack sufficient official evidence here, including Brambleghast's evolution, and remain unreleased. Search failure is an **unresolved evidence gap**, not proof that a Pokémon cannot exist in GO. All gaps are in [codex-questions.md](../../codex-questions.md).

Added **13 dated entries**, preserving all six existing ledger forms. Toxel and Sinistea change Normal availability; Maschiff/Mabosstiff and Flamigo already had reviewed corrections. Added the Applin family's Shiny availability. No Shadow/Purified category, future release, new form ID or schema was invented. Legacy windows are unchanged; reviewedAt advances to September 30.

The authoritative inputs are the repository's generated catalog plus reviewed overlay and dated ledger. The workbook is only import evidence. Artwork, workbook marks, main-series Pokédex pages, event teasers, search snippets and generic Rocket mentions do not establish GO availability. Ledger dates use the announced first obtainable day, not publication day. Date-only entries preserve the existing local-time model; no timezone redesign is part of L1.

Normal releases receive existing tradeable Lucky and IV/size eligibility. Gender categories follow the app's existing gender rules, without ledger gender entries. The official [XXS/XXL announcement](https://pokemongo.com/news/more-xxs-xxl-pokemon-in-pokemon-go) covers all GO Pokémon, and the [trading help](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/96-trading-pokemon/) preserves Mythical restrictions. No newly released item here is Mythical. Shiny gets a separately sourced date. Evolved shinies are **obtainability inferences** from the base shiny debut plus an officially documented evolution, allowed by the task; the posts need not separately announce every shiny evolution.

Toxel's November 18 date is the verified **global** Into the Wild debut, with Shiny available at that start. An earlier regional debut was not established from official material in this review; Q4 records that limit rather than inventing a date. Sinistea's December 3 normal debut and October 21, 2025 shiny debut are separately documented. Polteageist is explicitly obtainable with 50 Sinistea Candy. For the Applin family, the two linked 2025 posts document the evolution paths, including Hydrapple's buddy requirement; sufficient Candy/items permit shiny evolutions from September 29, 2026.
