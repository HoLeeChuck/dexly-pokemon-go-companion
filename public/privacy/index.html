Privacy notice

CatchGrid is private by default. Public users do not create an account, and their collection, settings, saved searches, and recovery snapshots are stored in that browser's site storage. This information does not automatically sync to another device.

Information CatchGrid handles

  • Your collection and preferences remain in your browser unless you export them.
  • CSV and backup files are processed only for the import or export you choose. Public browser imports are not uploaded to a CatchGrid account.
  • The unlisted owner mode stores one private administrator collection in Cloudflare D1. It is separate from public browser collections and protected by an access secret.
  • Cloudflare processes normal request information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, request path, browser information, diagnostic identifiers, and timestamps. CatchGrid's Worker logs do not intentionally record collection bodies or access secrets.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics may collect privacy-focused aggregate usage and performance measurements. CatchGrid does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Control and deletion

Use CatchGrid's export tools before changing browsers or clearing site data. Removing this site's stored data in your browser deletes its local collection from that browser. An exported file is controlled by you and must be deleted from your own files separately.

Third-party images

Pokémon sprite requests may be served from GitHub's raw-content service. Those requests are governed by GitHub's privacy practices. See CatchGrid's third-party notices for the asset-source limitations.

Questions

Use the project's public issue tracker for privacy questions that do not contain sensitive information. Report security issues privately through the security channel.
